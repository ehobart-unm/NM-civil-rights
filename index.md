New Mexico is a multiethnic state - a majority-minority state shaped by a large Hispanic/Latino/Chicanx population, a substantial Indigenous (Native American) population, and a non-Hispanic white population.  New Mexico is one of only two states in the United States (the other being California) where the largest single demographic group is Hispanic/Latino/Chicanx.

# New Mexican Civil Rights in the 20th century

<div class="story-box-row">

  <div class="story-box">

    <h3>During the twentieth century, the following events only happened in New Mexico:</h3>

    <ul>
      <li>In 1912, New Mexico became the 47th state in the union.</li>
      <li>In 1916, Pancho Villa invaded the United States at Columbus, New Mexico.</li>
      <li>In 1945, The first nuclear weapon in the world was tested.</li>
      <li>In 1947, An unidentified flying object (UFO) allegedly crashed at Roswell, setting off a craze about aliens around the country.</li>
      <li>In 1980, The Duran decree was signed.</li>
    </ul>
